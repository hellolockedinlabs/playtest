How to play

Playtest guide · prototype

Hexbrew

You run a crew of magical troublemakers, and tonight's target is someone else's café. Send them in, cast spells, and break the place before the timer runs out. The café fights back on its own: its staff and defenses react to everything you do.

There's no art yet. Everything is circles, squares and letters. Judge the decisions and the tension, not the looks.

The goal: up to 3 stars

Ruin its reputation

Reputation starts at 100%. Unhappy customers walking out and customers turned away from a full queue push it down. Get it to 0.

Rob the vault

A Locksmith has to pick the vault (T3) and get back out the window. If staff catch it on the way out, the loot goes back.

Overwhelm the staff

Break more than the staff can handle. The Overwhelm bar fills while any problem has nobody on it and drains when they catch up. Star at .

How a raid goes

  1. Setup

    Pick a café level and build your army. The army has space, and each card uses some of it. Leave the staff priorities and the seed as they are.

  2. Scout , free

    Look at the layout, the staff and the entry points. Traps stay hidden. Tap Start siege when you're ready.

  3. Siege

    Tap a troublemaker card, then tap a highlighted entry point to send it in. Once it's in, you can't steer it. Tap a spell card, then tap anywhere on the map to cast it. Every card costs Mischief, which holds up to and refills by 1 every . Choosing when to spend it is the main skill.

  4. Closing ~5 s

    The café closes and everyone still inside walks out. Customers in a bad mood still cost reputation on the way out, so a raid can win a star right at the end.

  5. Result

    Stars and loot. Try the same café again or move up a level.

Your troublemakers

Each one does one thing and enters through one kind of entry point.

R

Crowd Rush

door · Mischief · space

A group of fake customers who fill the queue with slow orders. While they wait, real customers lose patience faster. They pass as customers, so staff can't throw them out. They sometimes drop food, which distracts the Warden Hound.

L

Locksmith

window · Mischief · space

Sneaks to the nearest unpicked lock and picks it: the register till (T1, , jams the register), the pantry stash (T2, ), or the vault (T3, ). Loot only counts if it escapes. A $ sack means it's carrying loot.

V

Vampire

chimney · Mischief · space

Latches onto a staff member and drains their Energy. Drained staff work slower, and at zero they faint for . It won't step into lantern light and can't latch onto Guard-first staff. It leaves after .

O

Poltergeist

any outer wall · Mischief · space

Drifts through walls and haunts the nearest working station (it turns blue), which then serves nothing until staff fix it. It haunts stations, then fades. Staff can't catch it while it's inside a wall.

Your spells

N

Neuroshock

cast anywhere · Mischief · space

Stuns every staff member in a small circle for , and they drop whatever they were doing. A stunned staff member can't be shocked again for , so you can't chain it.

B

Blackout

cast anywhere · Mischief · space

Kills the lights in an area for . Staff inside move slower and only notice trouble right next to them. Lanterns in the area go out, so a Vampire can get in. The Warden Hound works by smell and isn't affected.

What defends the café

The owner isn't online. Everything below runs on its own.

Staff (3, shown as green S / F / G)

S · Serve first

Keeps customers happy. Takes Crowd Rush orders at normal speed.

F · Fix first

Repairs haunted and jammed stations fast.

G · Guard first

Spots and throws out intruders from farther away. Vampires can't latch onto them.

Staff do their own job at full speed and other jobs at . The bar under each staff member is their Energy.

W

Warden Hound

visible

A guard dog that patrols its zone (the dashed brown area). It sniffs out nearby troublemakers so staff can see them, even in a Blackout. If it reaches a Locksmith, it pins it for and barks for a guard (WOOF). Dropped food distracts it.

T2

Mimic

hidden trap

A fake pantry stash that looks exactly like the real one. When a Locksmith tries to pick it, it grabs the Locksmith for and eats any loot it's carrying. It works once.

☀

Lantern

visible · warm tiles

Lights the area around the counter. Vampires won't enter the light and let go of anyone who walks into it. A Blackout puts it out for a while.

!

Alarm Bell

hidden trap

Hidden in the vault room. The first Locksmith or Poltergeist to touch it alerts every staff member at once. It works once.
