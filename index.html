How to play

Playtest guide · prototype

Hexbrew

You run a crew of magical troublemakers, and tonight's target is someone else's café. Send them in, cast spells, and break the place before the timer runs out. The café fights back on its own: its staff and defenses react to everything you do.

There's no art yet. Everything is flat tiles and emoji. Judge the decisions and the tension, not the looks.

The goal: up to 3 stars

Ruin its reputation

Reputation starts at 100%. Customers who give up and walk out, and customers turned away because the queue is full or there is no table, push it down. Get it to 0.

Rob the vault

A Locksmith has to pick the vault (💎) and get back out the window. If staff catch it on the way out, the loot goes back.

Overwhelm the staff

Break more than the staff can handle. The Overwhelm bar fills while any problem has nobody on it and drains when they catch up. Star at .

How a raid goes

  1. Pick a raid

    The first three raids are guided and add one card each. After them, each café adds one defense. Your army is chosen for you.

  2. Scout , free

    Look at the layout, the staff and the entry points. Traps stay hidden. Tap Start siege when you're ready.

  3. Siege

    Tap a troublemaker card, then tap a highlighted entry point to send it in. Once it's in, you can't steer it. Tap a spell card, then tap anywhere on the map to cast it. Every card costs Mischief, which holds up to and refills by 1 every . Choosing when to spend it is the main skill.

  4. Closing ~5 s

    The café closes and everyone still inside walks out. Customers in a bad mood still cost reputation on the way out, so a raid can win a star right at the end.

  5. Result

    Stars, and one line on why you missed each one. Try again or move on.

Your troublemakers

Each one does one thing and enters through one kind of entry point.

👥

Crowd Rush

take-out door · Mischief · space

A group of fake customers who fill the take-out queue with slow orders. While they wait, real customers lose patience faster. They pass as customers, so staff can't throw them out. They sometimes drop food, which distracts the Warden Hound.

🪑

Table Hoggers

dining door · Mischief · space

A group of fake diners who each take a table, order slowly and then sit there for . Real diners find no table, wait, and leave angry. Each one leaves a dirty table behind. Staff can't throw them out.

🥷

Locksmith

window · Mischief · space

Sneaks to the nearest unpicked lock and picks it: the register till (💵, , jams the register), the pantry stash (📦, ), or the vault (💎, ). Loot only counts if it escapes. A 💰 sack means it's carrying loot.

🧛

Vampire

chimney · Mischief · space

Latches onto a staff member and drains their Energy. Drained staff work slower, and at zero they faint for . It won't step into lantern light and can't latch onto Guard-first staff. It leaves after .

👻

Poltergeist

any outer wall · Mischief · space

Drifts through walls and haunts the nearest working station (it turns blue), which then serves nothing until staff fix it. It haunts stations, then fades. Staff can't catch it while it's inside a wall.

😈

Mess Gremlin

dining door · Mischief · space

Makes messes and runs off. It dirties empty tables, so nobody can sit there until staff clean them. With no table left it spills on the floor, and customers wade through the spill slowly. Staff can throw it out.

🧐

Picky Critic

dining door · Mischief · space

One fake diner in a hurry. If it gets no table within , or no food within of sitting down, it writes a bad review: − reputation. Staff can't throw it out, but a Guard-first staff member can spot it, and then it is served first.

Your spells

⚡

Neuroshock

cast anywhere · Mischief · space

Stuns every staff member in a small circle for , and they drop whatever they were doing. A stunned staff member can't be shocked again for , so you can't chain it.

🌑

Blackout

cast anywhere · Mischief · space

Kills the lights in an area for . Staff inside move slower and only notice trouble right next to them. Lanterns in the area go out, so a Vampire can get in. The Warden Hound works by smell and isn't affected.

🧲

Lure

cast anywhere · Mischief · space

For , staff and the Warden Hound nearby drop everything and walk to the spot. Customers in a line nearby step out and go to the back afterwards. Guard-first staff ignore it, and lured staff can't be lured again for .

⏰

Rush Hour

cast anywhere · Mischief · space

For , real customers pour in at both doors. A café that is keeping up just earns more; one with broken stations or clogged lines drowns in angry customers.

What defends the café

The owner isn't online. Everything below runs on its own.

Staff (3 to 6, more in bigger cafés)

🧑‍🍳 Serve first

Keeps customers happy: takes orders, brings food to tables and cleans them. Takes Crowd Rush and Table Hogger orders at normal speed.

🧑‍🔧 Fix first

Repairs haunted and jammed stations fast.

💂 Guard first

Spots and throws out intruders from farther away. Vampires can't latch onto them.

Staff do their own job at full speed and other jobs at . The bar under each staff member is their Energy.

🐕

Warden Hound

visible

A guard dog that patrols its zone (the dashed brown area). It sniffs out nearby troublemakers so staff can see them, even in a Blackout. If it reaches a Locksmith, it pins it for and barks for a guard. Dropped food distracts it.

📦

Mimic

hidden trap

A fake pantry stash that looks exactly like the real one. When a Locksmith tries to pick it, it grabs the Locksmith for and eats any loot it's carrying. It works once.

🏮

Lantern

visible · warm tiles

Lights the area around the counter. Vampires won't enter the light and let go of anyone who walks into it. A Blackout puts it out for a while.

🔔

Alarm Bell

hidden trap

Hidden in the vault room. The first Locksmith or Poltergeist to touch it alerts the nearest staff members at once. It works once.

🚫

Door policy

visible · 🚫 on a door

When the queue reaches , or every table and the waiting line are full, the café stops letting real customers in. They go elsewhere and nobody gets angry. Crowd Rush, Table Hoggers and the Picky Critic push in anyway.

Café levels

Every café is the same size. After the three guided raids, each one has more in it: more staff, more tables, more customers, and one new defense.

Level 1No defenses. 3 staff, all Serve first. 4 tables.
Level 2One staff switches to Fix first.
Level 34 staff, one of them Guard first. 5 tables.
Level 4+ Warden Hound. 6 tables.
Level 5+ Mimic. 5 staff.
Level 6+ Lantern. 7 tables.
Level 7+ Alarm Bell (the full café). 6 staff, 8 tables.

Reading the screen

💎 😡 ❗
Your goals, at the top. Each turns gold when you win it. The 😡 bar is the café's reputation (your target is 0); the ❗ bar is Overwhelm.
The map
Drag to move it, pinch to zoom.
Red !
A problem nobody is handling: a broken station or a fainted staff member. Overwhelm fills while one is showing.
✨ Mischief
Your spending meter. It refills on its own.
🙂 😐 😠
Customers, losing patience. 😎 are your Crowd Rush and Table Hoggers.
🥡 and 🍽️
The two customer doors. Take-out customers (🥡) queue at the register. Dine-in customers (🍽️) sit at a table, order (✋), eat and leave.
Dark table
A dirty table with dishes on it. Nobody can sit there until a staff member cleans it. 🪴 is a spot with no table yet.
💫 / 💤
A staff member who is stunned or fainted.
Dark disc
A Blackout in progress.
⏸, 1×, ✕
Pause the raid, change its speed, or end it (✕ twice). Use them whenever you like.

For playtesters

  • Start at raid 1; each raid you win opens the next.
  • Say what you're thinking out loud: what you're trying, what surprised you, what annoyed you.
  • If you want to stop, say so. If you want one more try, say that too. Both answers help.
